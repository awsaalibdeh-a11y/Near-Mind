    How NearMind works

    A robot on every roof helps neighbors share, without anyone giving up their privacy.

    NearMind City: 5 neighborhoods · 20 buildings · 200 families · 1,182 people. Each building has its own robot that only connects the 10 families inside it.

    The problem

    People buy things they need once (a drill, a ladder) while the neighbor next door has one. Asking the whole building is awkward and noisy, so nobody asks.

    The idea

    Every message goes to NearMind first. It works out what you need, asks only the 1-3 neighbors most likely to help, and arranges the handover at your door. You can also sell things to the neighbors who'd actually want them.

    1. UnderstandAn AI model turns a casual message into a structured request: item, how many, how urgent, or a sale price.
    2. RouteIt picks who to ask from public facts only (job, hobbies, who's awake). It never sees what's inside anyone's home.
    3. ClarifyVague replies ("the long thing in the garage") get one follow-up question before they reach you.
    4. Hand overYou choose; NearMind confirms a time at a door and thanks everyone else.

    The AI inside

    • Router: one language model that replies in strict JSON (what it thinks, who to message, status changes). The page animates every step.
    • Neighbors: each of the ten is its own simulated person with a private inventory, personality, sleep hours and budget, so the router must actually ask to find out.
    • Real pilot: the same robot was run over WhatsApp with real neighbors (switched off in this demo).

    Responsible by design

    • Privacy: neighbors never see each other's chats; only the asker hears the offers.
    • Asks as few people as possible: the counter shows how many were left in peace.
    • Respects sleep: people who are probably asleep aren't pinged unless it's urgent; quiet hours 22:00-08:00 on WhatsApp.
    • Never starts a chat: on WhatsApp it only answers people who opted in.
    • Honest: it only relays what neighbors actually said and flags unsafe offers.
    • Humans decide: the asker always picks; selling has no pressure.
